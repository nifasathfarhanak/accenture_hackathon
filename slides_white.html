The Banking Compliance Crisis

Banks spend billions annually manually reconciling thousands of regulatory obligations across DORA, OCC 2011-12, Basel III, and MAS guidelines.

⚠️ Fragmented & Manual Reconciliations

Compliance officers spend 60-70% of their time cross-referencing multi-hundred-page circulars against disparate internal control spreadsheets.

  • Slow, error-prone manual Excel matrices
  • High turnover among compliance analysts
  • Inability to keep pace with changing circulars
❌ Black-Box AI Hallucinations

Generic LLMs hallucinate control adequacy, miss critical sub-clauses, and lack deterministic explainability required by regulatory auditors.

  • No grounded citations to exact circular paragraphs
  • Risk of blind approval for non-compliant controls
  • Regulators reject unexplainable AI mappings
⚖️ Zero Audit Trail & Enforcement

Auditors from FED, ECB, or RBI demand tamper-evident historical proof of who approved, modified, or flagged gaps in every single control.

  • Approval rationales are missing or informal
  • Gap closures are not tracked with action plans
  • Regulatory audit failure carries severe fines

REGULATRACE AI Solution Overview
03 / 12

The RegulaTrace AI Solution Architecture

A high-precision, Agentic Workbench designed specifically for Tier-1 banking compliance teams.

✨ Three-Pillar Agentic Workflow
  • Agent 1: Intelligent Ingestion & Clause Extractor
    Parses unstructured policy PDFs & text (DORA ICT, OCC 2011-12, Basel III) and generates atomic, traceable obligations with exact citations.
  • Agent 2: Multi-Factor Semantic Matcher
    Synthesizes full text, semantic intent, and operational domain to match controls with a composite 4-tier confidence score.
  • Agent 3: Autonomous Gap & Defect Detector
    Identifies missing requirement components (people, process, technical controls) and demands explicit remediation.
🛡️ Strict Human-In-The-Loop Governance

AI suggests; humans decide. RegulaTrace AI enforces banking-grade verification checkpoints before any mapping enters production:

  • Mandatory Rationale Validation: Minimum 10-character rationale required to approve any control mapping.
  • Gap Acknowledgment Enforcement: If a control is Partial or Gap, system blocks approval until remediation plans are signed off.
  • Audit Trail Immutability: Every action, edit, RFI, and review generates an append-only, tamper-evident audit log.

07 / 12

Autonomous Gemini Ingestion Engine

Live extraction of atomic compliance obligations from raw text using Google Gemini 1.5 Flash.

⚡ Live AI Ingestion Pipeline

Analyst pastes or uploads raw regulatory documents (PDF/Text). The agent performs zero-shot structured extraction:

  • Parses complex circular clauses into atomic obligations
  • Assigns regulatory IDs (e.g., DORA-ICT-01, OCC-MR-04)
  • Breaks down mandatory sub-components (policies, logging, crypto)
  • Generates confidence score & compliance guidance notes
💡 Resilient Dual-Mode Execution

Built for enterprise resilience with zero single point of failure:

  • Live API Mode: Connects directly to Gemini 1.5 Flash via user-configured API key in UI settings.
  • Autonomous Mock Engine: Intelligent domain-specific parser activates automatically when offline or in air-gapped demo environments.
  • Pre-Built Presets: Instant one-click sample circulars (DORA ICT Resilience, OCC Model Risk, Basel III, MAS TRMG).
